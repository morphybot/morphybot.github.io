Branding Premium

The bot that becomes yours

Staff run /branding to give the bot its own name, avatar, banner and About Me in their server. Every other server keeps its own version, so someone in two servers sees two different bots.

  • Name up to 32 characters
  • About Me up to 190 characters
  • Avatar and banner: PNG, JPG, GIF or WEBP, up to 8 MB
  • /branding reset brings back Morphy's look

If Premium ends, the bot goes back to Morphy's own name and look until you renew.

How to get Premium

How paying works

Run /subscribe in your server to see how to pay. After you pay, run it again and press I've paid. We check each payment by hand and switch Premium on within 24 hours. Payments don't renew on their own, so you pay again when you want more time.

If Premium ends

Premium tools pause and the bot goes back to Morphy's own name and look until you renew. The Free plan keeps working, and your settings stay saved.

No refunds for time already paid, except for a billing mistake or where required by law. To report a billing mistake or ask about a refund, open a ticket in the Morphy Support server or email morphybot.support@gmail.com.

Straight answers

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. Custom commands, moderation, welcome and goodbye messages, picture welcome cards, auto roles, role menus and XP levels keep working with no card and no end date.

How do I pay for Premium?

Run /subscribe in your server to see how to pay. After you pay, run it again and press I've paid. A monthly payment adds 30 days and a yearly one adds 365. We check each payment by hand and switch Premium on within 24 hours. Payments don't renew on their own. Questions go in a ticket in the Morphy Support server.

Does each server pay on its own?

Yes. Premium is per server, so two servers need two plans. Paying for one doesn't change the other.

What happens when Premium ends?

Premium tools pause, and the bot goes back to Morphy's own name and look until you renew. The Free plan keeps working and your settings stay saved.

Can I get a refund?

No refunds for time already paid, except for a billing mistake or where required by law. If something went wrong with a payment, open a ticket in the Morphy Support server or email morphybot.support@gmail.com.

Is there a website dashboard?

Yes. Sign in with Discord to see the servers where you're the owner or have Manage Server. It changes the same settings as the slash commands.

Does Morphy need Administrator?

No. It asks only for the permissions its features use, and never for Administrator. See the list.

Does it connect to my game server?

Only to read it. With Premium, /servers add tracks an ARK server's status and players with no login, and /nitrado link adds game logs. Morphy can't restart, stop or change a server.

Are the games real-money gambling?

No. Blackjack, slots, roulette and the other games use free, make-believe coins. Nobody can buy coins through Morphy or cash them out, and the Terms don't allow trading coins or winnings for anything of value. The games come with Premium, which pays for the bot. It doesn't buy coins or prizes, and it doesn't change the odds.

Will my server show up on this website?

Yes, by default, with its name, icon and member count. Staff can hide it any time with /showcase off and bring it back with /showcase on.
