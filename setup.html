Getting started with Morphy

Morphy is the all-in-one Discord bot that becomes yours. Welcomes and roles are free. Premium adds tickets, invites, giveaways, applications, info panels, community games with make-believe coins, and the bot in your server's own name and look.

Every server gets the Free plan: welcome messages and picture cards, goodbye messages, auto roles and role menus. It costs nothing and doesn't run out. Premium adds everything else, including your server's own name and look for the bot, for $9.99 USD a month (30 days) or $99.90 USD a year (365 days) per server. Steps 2 and 4 to 10 below need Premium.

Everything is set up with slash commands: type / and pick one. Setup commands only show for staff with the right permission, and every setting only affects your server. For the full list of commands, see the commands page or type /help in Discord.

Need help? Open a ticket in the Morphy support server: https://discord.gg/Mp8c7ra6dx

Step 1: Add Morphy and check it's healthy

  1. Invite Morphy with the Add Morphy free button on https://morphybot.github.io. Your server starts on the Free plan, and the server owner gets a DM with how to begin. No card is needed.
  2. In Server Settings → Roles, drag Morphy's role above any roles you want it to hand out (auto roles, role menus, application roles). Morphy can only give roles below its own.
  3. Run /setup. It shows:
    • your plan (Free or Premium)
    • whether Morphy is missing any permissions
    • which features are on or off
    • ⚠️ next to a feature whose channel was deleted, or where Morphy can't post (it names the channel and the missing permission)
  4. Pick a private staff channel for Morphy's activity log: /setup log_channel:#staff-log.

Step 2: Make it look like your server (Premium)

  • /branding name sets the bot's name in your server, e.g. "Community Helper".
  • /branding avatar and /branding banner upload your logo and banner.
  • /branding bio sets the About Me.
  • /branding color sets the color stripe on most of the bot's posts, like /help, tickets, role menus, polls and giveaways. Use a hex code like #FF8800. Casino replies, info panels and announcements keep their own colors.
  • /branding footer adds a short line at the bottom of /help, tickets, role menus and the other posts that use your color. Polls and giveaways keep their own footer line.

Members will see your branding, not Morphy's. /branding reset puts it back. If Premium ends, Morphy goes back to its own look in your server and keeps your branding saved. It comes back when Premium starts again.

Your server also shows up on Morphy's website, https://morphybot.github.io, with its name, icon and member count, on either plan (age-restricted servers are never listed). Servers on the Free plan are in a separate "Also using Morphy" group, below the servers with Premium. /showcase off hides it, /showcase invite adds a Join button, and /showcase status shows what's listed. These work on the Free plan too.

Step 3: Welcome new members (Free plan)

  1. /welcome channel turns welcomes on in your welcome channel.
  2. /welcome message edits the text. You can use placeholders:
    • {user} pings the new member in the line above the card. Inside the card it shows their name instead, since Discord often shows pings there as a long number.
    • {username} is their name
    • {server} is your server's name, {membercount} is the member count
    • {#rules} becomes a clickable link to that channel
    • {inviter} is who invited them and {invites} is that person's invite count. These need Premium, since invite tracking is a Premium feature. On the Free plan they show "someone" and 0.
  3. /welcome banner adds a banner image to the card (optional). Keep it under 8 MB and 4096 pixels wide or tall.
  4. /welcome card on:True turns on picture cards. Each new member gets their own picture: your banner on top, then their avatar, "Welcome, <name>" and their member number. Picture cards are off until you turn them on. No banner? The picture card uses your welcome color. Animated banners show as a still picture on it.
  5. /welcome test shows a preview using yourself.
  6. Optional extras:
    • /goodbye channel posts a message when someone leaves.
    • /autorole add gives a role to everyone who joins.
    • /rolemenu create, then /rolemenu add and /rolemenu post, lets members pick their own roles, like ping roles or platforms.

Step 4: Tickets (Premium)

  1. /tickets preset loads a ready-made set of ticket types. Pick the one closest to your server:
    • a general community set (Support, Partnership, Promotion, Report) that suits most servers
    • a game-server set (Discord help, Shop, In-game help, Report, Invite reward, Booster reward)

    You can rename, add or remove any type afterwards, so start with whichever is closer.

  2. /tickets staff-add lets your staff roles see and handle tickets.
  3. /tickets settings sets where ticket channels open, where transcripts go, and whether quiet tickets auto-close.
  4. /tickets panel posts the ticket panel in your support channel.

How it works:

  • A member picks a type and answers the form, and a private channel opens for them and your staff.
  • Staff press Claim to take the ticket (only the claimer can type) and Staff info to see the member's history.
  • Staff or the member press Close to close it. A transcript is saved to your transcript channel. If Morphy can't save it (for example it can't read the ticket's messages or post files in that channel), the ticket stays open and says what to fix, so no conversation is lost.
  • Change types with /tickets type-add and /tickets question-add.

Ping different staff for different ticket types (optional):

  • /tickets ping-add type:Shop role:@Shop Staff makes new Shop tickets ping only @Shop Staff, and lets that role see, claim and close Shop tickets (not other types).
  • A type can ping up to 5 roles. Types without their own ping roles keep the normal staff ping (/tickets settings ping_staff).
  • /tickets ping-remove takes a role off again, and /tickets list shows who each type pings. Tickets that are already open keep their access; the role just can't claim or close them any more.
  • Discord only delivers a role ping if the role has Allow anyone to @mention this role turned on (Server Settings › Roles). Morphy tells you when it's off.

Step 5: Safety (Premium)

  • /honeypot setup turns a channel into a trap for hacked accounts. Anyone who posts there is banned (or kicked) automatically and their spam is deleted. Staff with Manage Messages are always safe, and /honeypot exempt-add protects other roles.
  • /antinuke on (server owner only) stops a hacked or rogue staff account from wrecking the server. If one person deletes 3 channels or roles, bans or kicks 5 members, gives a role Administrator-type permissions, or adds a bot, all within a minute, Morphy removes all their roles (or bans them), takes the dangerous permissions back off, kicks any bot they added, and DMs you. /antinuke status shows the limits and warns you if Morphy's role isn't at the top of the role list (it must be, to stop anyone). Trust co-owners and helper bots with /antinuke trust. Change limits with /antinuke limit.
  • /purge bulk-deletes recent messages in a channel.

Step 6: Community (Premium)

  • Invite tracking shows who invited each new member:
    • /invites-setup channels picks where "X joined, invited by Y" posts go.
    • /invites-setup tiers sets rewards, e.g. 5 = a custom role or 10 = a shout-out in announcements.
    • Members check their count with /invites check. Fake (brand-new) accounts and people who leave don't count.
  • /boosts setup turns on booster thank-yous, then /boosts text sets the message and reward.
  • /suggestions setup picks the suggestions channel. Members use /suggest and vote with buttons.
  • /poll create makes a poll with buttons and an auto-close timer.
  • /autoreact add reacts with up to 3 emojis to every post in a channel, to posts with a word or phrase, or to that word in one channel only. Words inside links and emoji names don't count. /autoreact list shows them and /autoreact remove stops one. A server can have up to 25. In a very busy chat Morphy skips some reactions so the rest don't show up late.

Step 7: Announcements, giveaways and info panels (Premium)

Announcements

  • /announce send starts from a ready-made template: Event, Update, Maintenance, Alert, General and more.
  • A form opens so you can fill in the [brackets]. Morphy won't post until they're filled.
  • Make your own templates with /announce template-edit.

Giveaways

  • /giveaway start starts one. Members press Enter, and winners are drawn automatically when time's up.
  • /giveaway reroll picks new winners.
  • If Morphy couldn't post the winner ping (or isn't sure it did), it won't ping twice. Your log channel gets a note and /giveaway list shows it for a week, so you can ping the winners yourself. The giveaway post always shows who won.
  • Save your usual giveaways with /giveaway template-edit.

Info panels

  • /panel edit fills in a starter panel: Rules, FAQ, Server info, Links and more.
  • /panel post posts it.
  • Editing a posted panel updates it instantly, and posting again moves it to the bottom of the channel.
  • Staff can also type ! plus the panel name, e.g. !rules, to drop a copy into any channel.

Payment links

  • /payments add method:Cash App link:$yourtag adds a way for members to pay you (Cash App, PayPal, Venmo, Stripe…). The emoji is optional, but it has to be a real one: pick it from Discord's emoji picker, or use one of your server's own, which looks like <:paypal:123456789012345678>. Anything else is refused instead of breaking the panel.
  • /payments post posts the panel. Members pick a method from the menu and get your link privately.
  • Staff can type !payments in any channel to post it.
  • Once you have at least one payment method, !links posts the payment panel, not the Links info panel, and Morphy says so when it does. For the info panel use /panel post, and for the payment panel use !payments. With no payment methods set, !links posts the Links info panel as usual.

Step 8: Staff applications (Premium)

  1. /applications create name:Staff review_channel:#staff-apps creates the form. You can also set a role to give accepted applicants and a role allowed to review.
  2. /applications questions changes the 5 starter questions. End a question with "(optional)" to make it skippable.
  3. /applications panel posts the Apply button.

How it works:

  • Members press Apply (or use /apply), and their answers arrive in the review channel.
  • Reviewers press Accept or Deny (with an optional reason).
  • The applicant gets a DM, and the role is given automatically on accept.
  • Denied members can re-apply after the cooldown (7 days by default, change it with /applications settings).
  • If Morphy can't post in the review channel (missing permissions), the application is kept, your log channel gets a note, and Morphy tries again every 5 minutes. /applications resend id:<number> posts it right away, or replaces a card that was deleted by mistake.

Step 9: Casino and economy (Premium, optional)

The games use make-believe coins. Nobody can buy them through Morphy or cash them out, and the Terms don't allow trading them for anything of value.

  1. /economy currency names your money, e.g. Coins 🪙 or Gems 💎.
  2. /economy payout, /economy cooldown and /economy bet-limit tune how fast people earn and how much they can bet.
  3. /economy toggle turns each game on or off, plus two earn commands that start switched off. /economy settings shows what's on.
  4. /economy channel-add limits the casino to certain channels.
  5. /economy role-income-add pays roles like boosters or VIPs on a timer.

Members can:

  • earn with /work, /crime and any other earn commands you switch on
  • move money with /deposit, /withdraw and /give-money
  • play /blackjack, /slot-machine, /roulette, /higher-lower, /russian-roulette and more
  • check balances with /money and /leaderboard

Everything above also works as a text command:

  • !bal, !dep all, !with 500, !give @member 100
  • !work, !crime, !collect, !lb
  • !bj all, !slots 100, !roulette 100 red, !hl 100, !rr 100

Change the ! with /economy prefix, or type off to turn text commands off.

Don't want anyone going below 0? /economy toggle feature:negative balances enabled:False makes fines only take the cash a member has.

Step 10: Points (Premium, optional)

Points staff hand out for events, competitions or good behavior, with your own name, e.g. Server points.

  1. /points setup name:Server points names them (and optionally picks a log channel).
  2. /points staff-add lets a role give points. Anyone with Manage Server can already.
  3. Staff use /points add, /points remove and /points add-role (for a whole team or role), each with an optional reason.

Members check their points with /points check and see the top members with /points leaderboard. Staff see a member's full history with /points history.

Step 11: Get Premium

Every server has the Free plan, with no time limit. Premium is $9.99 USD a month (30 days) or $99.90 USD a year (365 days) per server.

  • Run /subscribe (needs Manage Server) to see what Premium adds and how to pay. Right now that's Cash App, Venmo or PayPal.
  • Put your server name in the payment note. Then run /subscribe again, press I've paid, pick Monthly or Yearly, and say how you paid and the name or note on the payment. We check each payment by hand and switch Premium on within 24 hours. With questions, open a Billing & payments ticket in the Morphy support server (https://discord.gg/Mp8c7ra6dx).
  • Each payment covers 30 days, or 365 days for the yearly price. You get a DM when Premium is on. Renewing early doesn't cost you days: the new days start when your current paid time ends.
  • The server owner gets a DM, and your log channel gets a post, 3 days before, 1 day before, the same day, and when paid time ends. Premium doesn't renew by itself, so keep your DMs open.
  • When paid time ends, Premium stays on for 3 more days so a late payment doesn't switch anything off. A payment made during those 3 days is still added to the old end date, so paying on the second of them leaves you about 28 days, not 30.
  • After that the server goes back to the Free plan. Welcome messages and picture cards, goodbye messages, auto roles and role menus keep working. The Premium features pause, anti-nuke and the do-not-post trap included, and the bot's name, picture, banner and About Me go back to Morphy's own look. All your settings are kept. When you pay again, the Premium features come back and Morphy puts back the branding it saved.

Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
A command doesn't show upPress Ctrl+R to refresh Discord. Setup commands only show for staff.
"Morphy is missing … permission"Give Morphy's role that permission, or allow it in that channel's settings.
Morphy can't give a roleDrag Morphy's role above that role in Server Settings → Roles.
Nothing happens in a channelCheck the channel doesn't block Morphy (channel settings → Permissions).
A feature stopped after you changed channels or rolesRun /setup again. It marks any channel that was deleted or that Morphy can't post in, and names the missing permission.
"This is a Morphy Premium feature."Your server is on the Free plan. Run /setup to see your plan, then /subscribe.
