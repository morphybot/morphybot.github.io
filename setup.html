Getting started with Morphy

Morphy is the all-in-one Discord bot that becomes yours. Welcomes and roles are free. Premium adds tickets, invites, giveaways, applications, info panels, community games with make-believe coins, and the bot in your server's own name and look.

Every server gets the Free plan: an account-age gate, custom commands, moderation and Automod, welcome messages and picture cards, goodbye messages, auto roles, role menus, chat and voice XP levels, a server log, reminders, birthdays, sticky roles and sticky messages. It costs nothing and doesn't run out. Premium adds everything else, including your server's own name and look for the bot, for $9.99 USD a month (30 days) or $99.90 USD a year (365 days) per server. Steps 2 and 4 to 10 below need Premium.

You set things up with slash commands: type / and pick one. Setup commands only show for staff with the right permission, and every setting only affects your server. For the full list of commands, see the commands page or type /help in Discord.

You can also use the web dashboard. On https://morphybot.github.io, click Dashboard and sign in with Discord. It lists the servers Morphy is in where you're the owner or have Manage Server, and it changes the same settings as the slash commands. Each step below names its dashboard page. Some things, like posting a giveaway or giving XP, are only in Discord, and the steps say so.

Need help? Open a ticket in the Morphy support server: https://discord.gg/Mp8c7ra6dx

Step 1: Add Morphy and check it's healthy

  1. Invite Morphy with the Add Morphy free button on https://morphybot.github.io. Your server starts on the Free plan, and the server owner gets a DM with how to begin. No card is needed.
  2. In Server Settings → Roles, drag Morphy's role above any roles you want it to hand out (auto roles, role menus, level roles, application roles). Morphy can only give roles below its own.
  3. Run /setup. It shows:
    • your plan (Free or Premium)
    • whether Morphy is missing any permissions
    • which features are on or off
    • ⚠️ next to a feature whose channel was deleted, or where Morphy can't post (it names the channel and the missing permission)
  4. Pick a private staff channel for Morphy's activity log: /setup log_channel:#staff-log.

On the dashboard, the Overview page shows your plan and Morphy's setup checks. The log channel is set in Discord only, with /setup log_channel.

Start from a server template (Premium)

/server-template preview adds a ready-made set of roles, categories and channels: Community, Support server or ARK cluster. /server-template list shows what's in each one.

  • The preview shows what Morphy would make and what's already there. Nothing changes until you press Confirm.
  • Morphy only adds what's missing. It never deletes, renames or moves a channel or role. A channel already counts as there when it has the same name, emojis and case aside, like rules for 📜│rules.
  • New roles go at the bottom of your role list with no permissions. Give them the permissions you want in Server Settings, Roles.
  • Staff-only categories are hidden from everyone except the template's staff role (Moderators, Support Team or Admins).
  • If Morphy's role is missing Manage Channels or Manage Roles, the preview names it and doesn't offer Confirm.
  • If you don't have a log channel yet, the template's morphy-logs channel becomes it.
  • /server-template history shows the last templates added and who added them.
  • On the dashboard, the Server templates page has the same preview and Confirm.

Step 2: Make it look like your server (Premium)

  • /branding name sets the bot's name in your server, e.g. "Community Helper".
  • /branding avatar and /branding banner upload your logo and banner.
  • /branding bio sets the About Me.
  • /branding color sets the color stripe on most of the bot's posts, like /help, tickets, role menus, polls and giveaways. Use a hex code like #FF8800. Casino replies, info panels and announcements keep their own colors.
  • /branding footer adds a short line at the bottom of /help, tickets, role menus and the other posts that use your color. Polls and giveaways keep their own footer line.

On the dashboard, use the Branding page.

Members will see your branding, not Morphy's. /branding reset puts it back. If Premium ends, Morphy goes back to its own look in your server and keeps your branding saved. It comes back when Premium starts again.

Your server also shows up on Morphy's website, https://morphybot.github.io, with its name, icon and member count, on either plan (age-restricted servers are never listed). Servers on the Free plan are in a separate "Also using Morphy" group, below the servers with Premium. /showcase off hides it, /showcase invite adds a Join button, and /showcase status shows what's listed. These work on the Free plan too.

/showcase is in Discord only.

Step 3: Welcome new members (Free plan)

  1. /welcome channel turns welcomes on in your welcome channel.
  2. /welcome message edits the text. You can use placeholders:
    • {user} pings the new member in the line above the card. Inside the card it shows their name instead, since Discord often shows pings there as a long number.
    • {username} is their name
    • {server} is your server's name, {membercount} is the member count
    • {#rules} becomes a clickable link to that channel
    • {inviter} is who invited them and {invites} is that person's invite count. These need Premium, since invite tracking is a Premium feature. On the Free plan they show "someone" and 0.
  3. /welcome banner adds a banner image to the card (optional). Keep it under 8 MB and 4096 pixels wide or tall.
  4. /welcome card on:True turns on picture cards. Each new member gets their own picture: your banner on top, then their avatar, "Welcome, <name>" and their member number. Picture cards are off until you turn them on. No banner? The picture card uses your welcome color. Animated banners show as a still picture on it.
  5. /welcome test shows a preview using yourself.

    On the dashboard, use the Welcome and goodbye page.

  6. Optional extras:
    • /goodbye channel posts a message when someone leaves.
    • /autorole add gives a role to everyone who joins.
    • /rolemenu create, then /rolemenu add and /rolemenu post, lets members pick their own roles, like ping roles or platforms.
    • On the dashboard, auto roles and role menus are on the Roles page.
    • /autorole sticky mode:on gives members their old roles back when they rejoin within 90 days. Roles with staff permissions, roles from bots and roles above Morphy's are never given back.

Reminders, birthdays and sticky messages (Free plan)

  • Any member can type /remindme in time:2h text:... or /remindme at time:18:30 text:... (US Central). The reminder comes back in that channel, or by DM with where:DM. /reminders list shows the ones waiting and /reminders cancel drops one. Up to 25 per member.
  • /birthday setup channel:#general turns on birthday posts. Add a message with {user} and {username}, a role members wear for the day, and a time zone name like America/New_York (US Central if you leave it out). Posts go out at midnight in that zone. Members add theirs with /birthday set month:10 day:20 (no year is kept) and /birthday list shows the next ones. /birthday off stops the posts.
  • /sticky set text:... keeps a message at the bottom of the channel: Morphy reposts it after every 5 messages (pick 2 to 50 with every) and deletes its previous copy. One per channel, 10 per server. /sticky remove takes it down.

XP and levels (Free plan)

Members earn XP when they chat and level up. It's off until you turn it on.

  1. /levels-setup on turns it on. By default each message gives 15 to 25 XP, once a minute per member. /levels-setup xp changes the amounts and the wait. Bots never earn XP.
  2. /levels-setup level-up picks where level-ups are posted (a channel, or where the member typed), turns the post off, or changes its text. You can use {user} {username} {level} {server}. Members aren't pinged unless you turn on ping.
  3. /levels-setup ignore-add stops XP in a channel (or a whole category) or for members with a role, like a bot-commands channel.
  4. /levels-setup role-add level:10 role:@Regular gives a role at a level. /levels-setup role-mode picks whether members keep their lower level roles or only the highest one. Morphy's role must be above these roles, and a level role can't also be an auto role or in a role menu, since Morphy takes level roles away again.
  5. /levels-setup weekly turns on a weekly board of the top 3. Pick the reset day (Monday by default, at midnight US Central), the channel, and rewards: Off, Manual (staff press Give on a card in the log channel) or Automatic. A reward can be a role for 7 days, casino coins (Premium only), or both. The first board posts after the first reset. Manual rewards can also be given or skipped with /levels-setup rewards.
  6. Members use /rank for their level card and /levels leaderboard for all time, this week, this month (from midnight US Central on the 1st) or time in voice. Staff use /xp give, /xp remove and /xp reset. XP staff give doesn't count toward the weekly board. /rank also puts on any level role the member's level earns but they don't have, and a member who leaves and comes back gets their level roles again.
  7. Voice XP is on as soon as levels are on: 5 to 10 XP per minute in a voice channel, for members who aren't muted or deafened, aren't alone (another person, not a bot, must be in the channel) and aren't in the AFK channel. Ignored channels, categories and roles apply here too. /levels-setup voice changes the amounts (1 to 100 a minute) or turns voice XP off.
  8. With Premium: /levels-setup multiplier-add times:2 role:@Booster or channel:#events gives more (or, below 1, less) XP to a role or in a channel or category, 0.5x to 3x, up to 10 roles and 10 channels; when more than one applies, the highest wins. /levels-setup boost times:2 hours:24 doubles XP for the whole server for a day (shown in /rank; off:true ends it early). /levels-setup coins-add level:10 coins:500 pays casino coins when a member reaches a level, once per member per level, only for XP earned by chatting or in voice.
  9. With Premium, /levels-setup card background:<picture> puts a picture under every /rank card (PNG, JPG or WEBP up to 2 MB, cut to the card's size), color:#1E90FF changes the card's accent, overlay:50 darkens the picture so the text stays readable (0 to 90 percent), and member_backgrounds:True lets members upload their own with /rank-card background:<picture> (/rank-card reset:True takes it away). /levels-setup card reset:True goes back to the plain look. On the Free plan every card has the plain look.
  10. Moving from another levels bot: export its leaderboard as JSON or a CSV with user id, xp and level columns, then run /levels-setup import source:<the bot> file:<the export>. Morphy shows how many members would get XP and how many are skipped (not in the server, or lines it can't read), then a Confirm button imports them in one go, up to 5,000 members. Merge (the default) never lowers anyone's XP; mode:replace writes the file's XP even when it's lower. Levels come from Morphy's curve, not the other bot's, so a member's level can change. The import goes to the log channel.

On the dashboard, use the XP and levels page for every /levels-setup setting, plus the leaderboards. Giving or skipping weekly rewards, /xp, the rank card look and the XP import are in Discord only.

Going from level L to the next takes 5 x L x L + 50 x L + 100 XP: 100 XP for level 1, 1,150 XP in all for level 5, 4,675 XP in all for level 10.

Server stats (every server)

Morphy counts a few numbers for your server each day: members, joins, leaves, messages, commands used, welcomes sent and voice joins. It counts only how many, never what was said or who did it. Staff with Manage Server can see them:

  1. /stats week shows the last 7 days, day by day, with each total compared with the week before.
  2. /stats export sends a CSV file of the daily numbers, 30 days by default and up to 400 (/stats export days:90).
  3. /stats tracking on:False stops the counting for your server, and on:True starts it again.

Days are US Central time, and new activity shows up within about 5 minutes. Numbers are kept for 400 days.

On the dashboard, the Analytics page has a chart, the same CSV and the counting switch.

Counter channels (every server)

A counter channel is a voice channel nobody can join whose name shows a number, like "Members: 412". Staff with Manage Channels set them up, and Morphy needs Manage Channels too.

  1. /counters add kind:members makes the channel. Kinds: members, humans, bots, boosts and ark-online (the players on your tracked ARK servers, so add one with /servers add first). Give it your own name with name:, keeping {count} in it ({max} too for ark-online), like name:Survivors: {count}.
  2. Morphy updates every counter every 10 minutes (Discord allows two renames per channel per 10 minutes). /counters refresh updates them now.
  3. /counters list shows them and /counters remove channel:#Members removes one and deletes its channel. Up to 10 per server. A counter whose channel you deleted by hand is forgotten on the next update.

There is no online-member counter: that needs the Presence intent, which Morphy does not ask for.

Automatic message rules (Free plan)

On the dashboard, open Moderation and safety to edit rules, actions and exemptions. The same rules can be changed in Discord.

Run /automod starter action:delete for a starter word list, or /automod add for words (including * wildcards), spam, duplicate text, mentions, caps, invites or zalgo. Set a mod-log with /moderation setup, then run /automod on. Free servers can add 10 rules; Premium allows 25, regex, attachment rules and exemptions for individual rules. /automod list, /automod pause and /automod remove manage saved rules. Server-wide exemptions are Free. Staff with moderation permissions are exempt. Morphy needs Manage Messages to delete messages and the appropriate permission for a timeout, kick or ban. A refused action is recorded in the mod-log. Regex rules that exceed their execution time limit are skipped. Spam and duplicate windows reset when Morphy restarts.

Server log (Free plan)

Morphy can post what happens in your server: message edits and deletes (with the deleted text when Morphy still had it), bulk deletes, joins, leaves, nickname changes, member role changes, server roles and channels made, deleted or renamed, voice joins, leaves and moves, bans and unbans, and timeouts. Bans and timeouts show who did it and the reason when Morphy's role has View Audit Log.

  1. Make a channel only staff and Morphy can see. A forum channel gets one post per event type, so edits, joins and bans each have their own thread. A text channel gets everything in one place.
  2. /logs setup channel:#server-log turns the log on. In a forum, Morphy makes each post the first time that kind of event happens, and makes it again if someone deletes it.
  3. /logs types type:Voice on:False turns one kind of event off. Every kind is on until you turn it off.
  4. /logs ignore channel:#bot-spam skips a channel or a whole category. Run it again to log it again.
  5. /logs status shows the setup, and /logs off turns the log off and keeps your choices.

Events that happen within 5 seconds are grouped into one message per type, so a raid or a purge is a few posts, not hundreds. Morphy's own messages, other bots' messages and the log channel itself are never logged. Members aren't pinged.

Pets (adopting is free, the rest is Premium)

Members adopt a virtual pet, feed it, play with it and level it up. Pets are on unless you turn them off.

  1. Members use /pet adopt to pick a kind (slime, cat, dog, fox and more) and a name. Each member can have one pet. /pet view shows it, and /pet release deletes it. These work on every plan.
  2. With Premium, /pet feed (+10 XP, once an hour by default) and /pet play (+15 XP, once every 30 minutes) level the pet up, and the card has Feed and Play buttons for its owner. A pet gets hungry and bored over time if nobody looks after it, but it never runs away. Level 2 takes 50 XP, level 5 takes 500 XP in all and level 10 takes 2,250. /pet leaderboard shows the pets with the most XP, and /pet rename changes a name.
  3. /pets-setup cards picks a channel for pet cards: one when someone adopts a pet and one when a pet levels up. Nobody is pinged. /pets-setup cooldowns changes how often members can feed and play.
  4. /pets-setup food price:25 makes each feed use one food that members buy with casino coins (/pet buy-food). A pet holds up to 50 food. The price is 0 by default, which means feeding is free.
  5. /pets-setup off turns pets off and keeps every pet. /pets-setup remove deletes one member's pet (for a name you don't want), and /pets-setup remove-all deletes every pet for a fresh start. These work on every plan.

On the dashboard, use the Pets page.

Step 4: Tickets (Premium)

  1. /tickets preset loads a ready-made set of ticket types. Pick the one closest to your server:
    • a general community set (Support, Partnership, Promotion, Report) that suits most servers
    • a game-server set (Discord help, Shop, In-game help, Report, Invite reward, Booster reward)

    You can rename, add or remove any type afterwards, so start with whichever is closer.

  2. /tickets staff-add lets your staff roles see and handle tickets.
  3. /tickets settings sets where ticket channels open, where transcripts go, and whether quiet tickets auto-close.
  4. /tickets panel posts the ticket panel in your support channel.

On the dashboard, the Tickets page has all of these settings and can post or update the panel.

How it works:

  • A member picks a type and answers the form, and a private channel opens for them and your staff.
  • Staff press Claim to take the ticket (only the claimer can type) and Staff info to see the member's history.
  • Staff or the member press Close to close it. A transcript is saved to your transcript channel. If Morphy can't save it (for example it can't read the ticket's messages or post files in that channel), the ticket stays open and says what to fix, so no conversation is lost.
  • Change types with /tickets type-add and /tickets question-add.

Ping different staff for different ticket types (optional):

  • /tickets ping-add type:Shop role:@Shop Staff makes new Shop tickets ping only @Shop Staff, and lets that role see, claim and close Shop tickets (not other types).
  • A type can ping up to 5 roles. Types without their own ping roles keep the normal staff ping (/tickets settings ping_staff).
  • /tickets ping-remove takes a role off again, and /tickets list shows who each type pings. Tickets that are already open keep their access; the role just can't claim or close them any more.
  • Discord only delivers a role ping if the role has Allow anyone to @mention this role turned on (Server Settings › Roles). Morphy tells you when it's off.

Staff pay (optional):

Morphy counts what each staff member handled in a month and adds up what that comes to under your own pay rates. It only does the math. It never sends, holds or asks for money.

  1. /staffpay setup currency:$ sets the label amounts show with ($, €, USD or your coin name). Add channel: to pick a text or forum channel for the monthly summary. A forum gets one post a month.
  2. /staffpay tier-set role:@Staff amount:0.20 pays @Staff 0.20 per ticket. Add ticket-type:Shop to set a different rate for one type, and per-application: to pay for reviewed applications. A member with more than one tier role is paid by the highest one in your role list.
  3. /staffpay summary shows this month so far, or pick another month. You get a CSV file with every number. Add post:true to post it in the summary channel.
  4. On the 1st of each month Morphy posts last month's summary in that channel by itself. Turn that off with /staffpay setup auto-post:false.
  5. Staff use /staffpay mine to see their own numbers.

On the dashboard, use the Staff pay page.

A ticket counts for the staff member who claimed it. A ticket closed without a claim counts for whoever closed it, unless the member closed it themselves or it closed automatically.

Step 5: Safety (Premium)

  • /honeypot setup turns a channel into a trap for hacked accounts. Anyone who posts there is banned (or kicked) automatically and their spam is deleted. Staff with Manage Messages are always safe, and /honeypot exempt-add protects other roles.
  • /antinuke on (server owner only) stops a hacked or rogue staff account from wrecking the server. If one person deletes 3 channels or roles, bans or kicks 5 members, gives a role Administrator-type permissions, or adds a bot, all within a minute, Morphy removes all their roles (or bans them), takes the dangerous permissions back off, kicks any bot they added, and DMs you. /antinuke status shows the limits and warns you if Morphy's role isn't at the top of the role list (it must be, to stop anyone). Trust co-owners and helper bots with /antinuke trust. Change limits with /antinuke limit.
  • /purge bulk-deletes recent messages in a channel. Filters: user, bots, humans, links, files and contains text.
  • On the dashboard, use the Moderation and safety page. Only the server owner can change anti-nuke there. /purge and actions against members are in Discord only.

Step 6: Community (Premium)

  • Invite tracking shows who invited each new member:
    • /invites-setup channels picks where "X joined, invited by Y" posts go.
    • /invites-setup tiers sets rewards, e.g. 5 = a custom role or 10 = a shout-out in announcements.
    • Members check their count with /invites check. Fake (brand-new) accounts and people who leave don't count.
  • /boosts setup turns on booster thank-yous, then /boosts text sets the message and reward.
  • Hype rewards give casino coins, a role, or both:
    • /hype name rewards members who put a keyword (like your server name) in their name here.
    • /hype tag rewards members who wear your server tag. Your server needs a tag first (Server Settings, Server Tag).
    • /hype boost rewards boosters and posts a thank-you.
    • /hype invite rewards members for each invite the invite tracker counts. Fake accounts and rejoins don't count, and each new member counts once.
    • /hype channel picks where the posts go. They don't ping anyone unless you turn ping on.
    • The cooldown is the hours before the same member can earn that reward again. 0 means once per season. /hype reset starts a new season; coins and roles from earlier seasons stay.
    • The name, tag and boost roles stay only while the member qualifies. The invite role stays for good.
    • /hype check looks at one member now. Members who already qualified before you turned a reward on get it the next time their name, tag or boost changes.
  • /suggestions setup picks the suggestions channel. Members use /suggest and vote with buttons. /suggestions options turns the DM to the author on a decision off or on, hides who suggested it (anonymous), and pings a staff role once when a suggestion reaches a number of upvotes (the role must allow anyone to @mention it).
  • /poll create makes a poll with buttons and an auto-close timer.
  • /autoreact add reacts with up to 3 emojis to every post in a channel, to posts with a word or phrase, or to that word in one channel only. Words inside links and emoji names don't count. /autoreact list shows them and /autoreact remove stops one. A server can have up to 25. In a very busy chat Morphy skips some reactions so the rest don't show up late.
  • On the dashboard, invite tracking and boosts are on the Growth page, hype rewards on Rewards and events, suggestions on Coins and community, and auto reactions on Commands and reactions. Polls are in Discord only.

Step 7: Announcements, giveaways and info panels (Premium)

Announcements

  • /announce send starts from a ready-made template: Event, Update, Maintenance, Alert, General and more.
  • A form opens so you can fill in the [brackets]. Morphy won't post until they're filled.
  • Make your own templates with /announce template-edit.
  • Announcements are in Discord only.

Embed builder

  • /embed create opens a private preview of your post with buttons: Title & text, Author & footer, Pictures, Add field, Add link button and Text & pings. The preview changes as you fill in each form.
  • A post can have up to 10 embeds and 5 link buttons. Use More to add another embed, remove one, import or export JSON, or start over.
  • Fill-ins: {server}, {membercount} and {#channel-name}.
  • Nothing pings unless you say so in Text & pings: none, or any of users, roles, everyone and role IDs. Only the text above the embeds can ping. Pinging everyone needs the Mention @everyone permission in that channel.
  • Picture links must be from an image host. Discord file links stop working after about a day, so Morphy refuses them.
  • Press Post and pick a channel to post it once. Press Save template to keep it for later: /embed post posts a saved template, /embed template-list lists them (up to 25 per server).
  • /embed edit with a message link loads a post Morphy made with /embed back into the builder. Save changes updates that post.
  • /embed import reads message JSON from other embed tools, and /embed export gives you a JSON file.
  • Your draft is saved, so you can close the preview and pick it up later with /embed create.
  • On the dashboard, use the Embeds page for saved templates and posting them.

Giveaways

  • /giveaway start starts one. Members press Enter, and winners are drawn automatically when time's up.
  • /giveaway reroll picks new winners.
  • If Morphy couldn't post the winner ping (or isn't sure it did), it won't ping twice. Your log channel gets a note and /giveaway list shows it for a week, so you can ping the winners yourself. The giveaway post always shows who won.
  • Save your usual giveaways with /giveaway template-edit.
  • On the dashboard, giveaway templates are on the Coins and community page. Starting a giveaway is in Discord only.

Info panels

  • /panel edit fills in a starter panel: Rules, FAQ, Server info, Links and more.
  • /panel post posts it.
  • Editing a posted panel updates it instantly, and posting again moves it to the bottom of the channel.
  • Staff can also type ! plus the panel name, e.g. !rules, to drop a copy into any channel.
  • On the dashboard, the Growth page edits panels and can update posts Morphy already made.

Payment links

  • /payments add method:Cash App link:$yourtag adds a way for members to pay you (Cash App, PayPal, Venmo, Stripe…). The emoji is optional, but it has to be a real one: pick it from Discord's emoji picker, or use one of your server's own, which looks like <:paypal:123456789012345678>. Anything else is refused instead of breaking the panel.
  • /payments post posts the panel. Members pick a method from the menu and get your link privately.
  • Staff can type !payments in any channel to post it.
  • Once you have at least one payment method, !links posts the payment panel, not the Links info panel, and Morphy says so when it does. For the info panel use /panel post, and for the payment panel use !payments. With no payment methods set, !links posts the Links info panel as usual.
  • On the dashboard, use the Growth page.

Step 8: Staff applications (Premium)

  1. /applications create name:Staff review_channel:#staff-apps creates the form. You can also set a role to give accepted applicants and a role allowed to review.
  2. /applications questions changes the 5 starter questions. End a question with "(optional)" to make it skippable.
  3. /applications panel posts the Apply button.

On the dashboard, use the Growth page for the forms. Reviewing applications is in Discord only.

How it works:

  • Members press Apply (or use /apply), and their answers arrive in the review channel.
  • Reviewers press Accept or Deny (with an optional reason).
  • The applicant gets a DM, and the role is given automatically on accept.
  • Denied members can re-apply after the cooldown (7 days by default, change it with /applications settings).
  • If Morphy can't post in the review channel (missing permissions), the application is kept, your log channel gets a note, and Morphy tries again every 5 minutes. /applications resend id:<number> posts it right away, or replaces a card that was deleted by mistake.

Step 9: Casino and economy (Premium, optional)

The games use make-believe coins. Nobody can buy them through Morphy or cash them out, and the Terms don't allow trading them for anything of value.

  1. /economy currency names your money, e.g. Coins 🪙 or Gems 💎.
  2. /economy payout, /economy cooldown and /economy bet-limit tune how fast people earn and how much they can bet.
  3. /economy toggle turns each game on or off, plus two earn commands that start switched off. /economy settings shows what's on.
  4. /economy channel-add limits the casino to certain channels.
  5. /economy role-income-add pays roles like boosters or VIPs on a timer.
  6. /economy daily sets what /play daily pays and the bonus for claiming every day (the streak resets after a missed Central calendar day). /economy chat-coins enabled:True pays a few coins per message on a cooldown; it's off by default.

Members can:

  • earn with /work, /play daily, /crime and any other earn commands you switch on
  • move money with /deposit, /withdraw and /give-money
  • play /blackjack, /slot-machine, /roulette, /higher-lower, /russian-roulette, /play coinflip, /play dice, /play rps and more
  • cash out before it's too late in /play crash (a climbing multiplier), /play mines (a grid with hidden mines, pick how many), /play tower (8 rows, one safe tile in three) and bet on /play race (4 animals, others join within 20 seconds, a winning pick pays 3.5x less the house edge). /economy house-edge sets the percent the house keeps in each of those four (4 by default)
  • challenge a member with /play duel: the other member accepts with a button within 60 seconds, both bets are held, and the winner takes both
  • check balances with /money and /leaderboard

Coin shop

Members spend their coins on things you sell: a role, a perk you hand out by hand, or anything else. Coins come out of their cash, never real money.

  1. /shop-setup add adds an item: a name, a price in coins, and if you want a description, a category, a role Morphy gives on buy, how many you have in stock, and the most one member can buy. An item can also need an XP level (min-level) or a role (require-role) before a member can buy it; Morphy checks that at the buy. Morphy won't sell a role with staff permissions or one above its own role.
  2. /shop-setup coupon-add makes a code for percent or coins off. You can cap its uses, end it after a time like 7d, set a minimum spend or tie it to one item. Each member can use a coupon once.
  3. /shop-setup settings names the shop, opens or closes it and picks the channel where every order and refund is posted. /shop-setup channel limits where members can buy.
  4. /shop-setup post posts a shop panel with an Open the shop button.

Members use /shop to look, and /buy or the menu under /shop to buy. Each buy shows the price first and needs a Buy press. /shop my-orders:True shows their own orders.

Staff see orders with /shop-setup orders, give coins back with /shop-setup refund (by default the stock and the coupon use come back and the role comes off), and download every order with /shop-setup export. If Discord won't give an item's role, the member gets their coins back right away.

#### Inventory and usable items

An item with keep:True goes into the member's inventory when they buy it instead of doing anything at once. Members see theirs with /inventory, use one with /use and, if the item is tradeable:True, hand it to someone with /give-item. Staff put an item in a member's inventory by hand with /shop-setup give (it goes in the order log).

What an item does is its actions text on /shop-setup add or edit, one action per line or after a ;, each starting with buy: (when they buy it) or use: (when they use it):

  • use: role @Role gives a role, and use: role @Role 7d takes it back after 7 days. role-days on the item does the same for every role it gives, the role option included.
  • buy: remove-role @Role takes a role off.
  • use: coins 100 adds 100 coins to their cash (times how many they use).
  • use: message {user} opened a {item}! posts in the channel; buy: dm Thanks for buying {item} in {server} sends a DM. {user}, {item} and {server} are filled in.
  • use: item Gold key x2 puts 2 of another item in their inventory.

An item with any use: action is kept in the inventory, so keep turns on by itself. A role Morphy can't give on buy refunds the order; on use, the item goes back into the inventory. Type - as the actions text to clear them. /shop-setup items and the shop show "kept", "usable" and "tradeable" on each item.

On the dashboard, use the Coin shop page for settings, items, coupons, orders and refunds. Posting the shop panel is in Discord only.

Guessing events

A guessing event runs in one channel. The first member to type the right answer wins casino coins. There are three kinds: a number (1 to 1000 unless you pick another range), a word or short phrase, and a vault code of 3 to 8 digits.

  1. /event setup channel:#guess-here picks the guess channel. Add ping-role to ping a role when an event starts. Discord only pings it if the role has "Allow anyone to @mention this role" turned on.
  2. /event setup also sets the prize: prize is the base, the pot grows by grow-by every grow-every wrong guesses (0 means it never grows), and top-prize is the most it can reach. cooldown is how many seconds a member waits between guesses (3 by default). /event setup with no options shows the settings.
  3. /event hosts action:Add role:@Event Team lets a role start and stop events. Members with Manage Server can always host.
  4. /event words action:Add text:raptor, rex, giga saves answers for word events. Morphy picks one at random when a host starts a word event without typing an answer.
  5. A host runs /event start kind:Number. Leave answer empty for a random answer that nobody sees, or type one. Only the host sees the command. The host's own guesses don't count.
  6. Members type their guesses in the guess channel. Messages that don't look like a guess, like chat in a word event, aren't counted as wrong guesses. Morphy posts when the pot grows and when someone wins.
  7. /event status shows the prize and the number of wrong guesses. /event stop ends the event and shows the answer, and nobody wins. /event stop works even if the server's Premium has ended.

One event runs at a time in a server. The guess channel can't change while an event runs in it. Hosts can't start an event with a prize above the top prize.

On the dashboard, use the Rewards and events page for the settings, hosts and answers, and to start or stop an event.

Custom income commands, coin draws and timed roles

Add your own earn commands next to /work: /economy income-add name:mine min:20 max:60 cooldown:30 makes !mine (and /job mine) pay 20 to 60 coins once every 30 minutes. Add role so only members with that role can use it, channel-only to keep it in one channel, and message to change the reply, with {user}, {amount} and {currency} filled in. A server can have 15. /economy income-list shows them and /economy income-remove takes one away. A name that's already a Morphy text command or a custom command is refused.

A coin draw sells tickets for coins and pays the pot to one ticket. /lottery start ticket-price:50 opens one in the channel you type it in; add max-tickets-per-member, ends-in (hours) to draw by itself, and pot-start to put coins in the pot. Members buy with /lottery buy and look with /lottery status. /lottery end draws now (the host or Manage Server). One draw runs at a time per server. With no tickets sold there's no winner.

/timedrole give member:@Someone role:@VIP days:7 gives a role that comes off by itself after 7 days (checked every 5 minutes). /timedrole list shows every timed role and /timedrole remove takes one off now. Needs Manage Roles, and Morphy won't give a role with staff permissions or one above its own.

Step 11: ARK server tracking (Premium, optional)

A live panel with your ARK servers: online or offline, map, players and version. Morphy only reads what the servers show publicly. It never asks for a game password, RCON or a host login.

  1. /servers add (needs Manage Server). Pick the game:
    • For ARK: Survival Ascended, start typing the server's name and pick it from the list, or type its IP and game port, like 1.2.3.4:7777. Morphy finds it on Studio Wildcard's public list of unofficial servers, so the server has to be on that list (official servers aren't covered).
    • For ARK: Survival Evolved (PC, Steam), type the IP and the query port, like 1.2.3.4:27015. If you leave the port out, Morphy tries 27015. Console servers can't be added this way.
    • Add a label to give it a shorter name on the panel.
  2. /servers panel posts the panel in the channel you pick. Morphy edits that same post when something changes, at most every 2 minutes. Posting it again moves it and removes the old one.
  3. /servers list shows everyone how the servers are doing, and /servers players shows who's on an ASE server. ASA's public list only has the player count, not names.

A server shows as offline after 2 checks in a row with no answer, so one lost packet doesn't flip it. If Morphy itself can't check (for example Wildcard's list is down), the panel says "Can't check right now" instead of offline. A Discord server can track up to 20 game servers. /servers remove stops one and deletes its history (Morphy keeps 30 days of online and player counts for each server). Without Premium, the checks stop and the panel says tracking is paused.

On the dashboard, the ARK servers page adds and removes servers, posts the panel and shows player charts. Linking Nitrado is in Discord only.

Game logs from Nitrado (Premium, optional)

If your game server is on Nitrado, Morphy can post its log lines in Discord channels. This needs a Nitrado link first: run /nitrado link and read the note it shows before you paste a token.

  1. /gamelogs channel (needs Manage Server). Pick a kind of line and a channel: joins, leaves, chat, kills, admin commands, server activity, or all of them. Several kinds can share one channel.
  2. /gamelogs status shows where each kind goes and when each game server was last read.
  3. /gamelogs limit sets how many lines Morphy posts an hour for your server (10 to 600, 120 to start). /gamelogs server turns one linked game server on or off, and /gamelogs pause and /gamelogs resume stop and start all of it.

Morphy reads Nitrado every 5 minutes and posts only lines that are new since it started reading, so turning it on never floods a channel with old lines. Posts never ping anyone, and player IDs (Steam, Epic, ARK IDs) are cut out. Morphy reads two things, both read-only: Nitrado's activity log for the server (restarts, settings and file changes, and who made them), which lands under server activity, and the newest part of an ARK server's ShooterGame.log, which gives joins, leaves, chat, kills and admin commands. Morphy reads no other file. If /gamelogs status says it couldn't find ShooterGame.log, check that the server is an ARK server on Nitrado and has been started at least once.

On the dashboard, use the Game logs page.

Raid alerts (Premium)

Use /raid setup threshold:10 window_seconds:60 cooldown_seconds:300 or open Moderation and safety, then Raid alerts. Choose an alert channel, or set the server log channel first. Morphy needs View Channel, Send Messages and Embed Links there. Alerts start off; setup enables them unless you choose enabled:false. A burst means the threshold was reached, not that every new member is malicious. Staff decide what to do next.

Use /raid status for the settings and last alert outcome, and /raid off to disable them. Turning alerts off and reading status remain available after Premium expires. Ticket follow-up tools (Premium)

Use /tickets settings closed_category to retain closed tickets (default 7 days). Without a closed category, the existing five-second deletion remains. Staff can /ticket reopen, /ticket rename or /ticket move. Give Morphy View Channel, Manage Channels and Manage Roles access in both categories. A refused move or permission change needs a staff retry; the reply tells you it did not finish.

/ticket close-request asks the opener to close or keep the ticket. Set close_request_hours for an optional deadline. /tickets reply-add saves text used by /ticket reply. /tickets block-add blocks new tickets by role; /tickets bypass-add skips only the open-ticket count, not the block or schedule. /tickets hours sets days, local open/close time, time zone, and whether to refuse or show a notice outside hours. /tickets settings dm_transcript:true sends transcript files to the opener when their DMs allow it.

Game logs in forum threads (Premium)

After linking Nitrado, use /gamelogs forum enabled:true channel:<forum> group:server to batch new entries by map. Group kind splits by event kind; group tribe splits named tribe events and leaves other entries in a general map thread. Give Morphy View Channel, Send Messages, Send Messages in Threads and Embed Links; Manage Threads is needed for archiving and reopening. The forum must permit posts without required tags. Existing text routes stay saved and resume with enabled:false. This reads logs only and never sends game commands.

/gamelogs trusted names:<comma-separated names> labels names on your list; names are not authenticated identities. /gamelogs signals enabled:true turns on optional review observations. Set a signals text channel or a forum to receive them. Scores indicate which observations to review, not a probability of cheating. First observed is not account age, a prior ban is not proof of evasion, and missed leave logs can look like long sessions. No automatic punishment occurs. Morphy keeps at most 50 saved thread mappings per map and forum, archiving the oldest when needed. The hourly cap still counts log lines, not embeds. Failed or capped posts can lose lines; this is not a durable forensic archive.
