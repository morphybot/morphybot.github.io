Morphy Terms of Service

Last updated: October 3, 2026

These terms cover your use of Morphy, a Discord bot run by Dakota ("we", "us"). By adding Morphy to a Discord server or using its commands, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please remove Morphy from your server.

1. What Morphy is

Morphy is a Discord bot with a free plan for every server and a paid plan called Premium. It provides tools such as tickets, welcome messages, applications, invite tracking, giveaways, polls, info panels, points and a casino game with make-believe money. Each server sets Morphy up for itself.

2. Who can use it

  • You must follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, including Discord's minimum age.
  • The person who adds Morphy to a server (or who manages it) is responsible for how it's set up and used in that server.

3. Free plan and Premium

  • Every server gets the Free plan: welcome messages and picture cards, goodbye messages, auto roles and role menus, plus the /setup, /help, /subscribe and /showcase commands. It has no time limit and needs no payment.
  • Premium adds the rest of Morphy, for example tickets, branding, the casino, applications, giveaways, invite tracking, anti-nuke and the do-not-post trap. It costs $9.99 per server for 30 days or $99.90 per server for 365 days (or the price shown when you pay).
  • Payments are made by hand for now (for example Cash App, PayPal or Venmo). Each one covers 30 or 365 days and doesn't renew automatically. When paid time ends, Premium stays on for 3 more days. If you pay before your paid time ends, or during those 3 days, the new days are added to the old end date. We'll remind you before the time runs out.
  • If we offer card payments, they renew automatically until you cancel with /subscribe, and Premium stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • When Premium ends, the server goes back to the Free plan. Premium features pause, and the bot's name, picture, banner and About Me in that server, and the color and footer on its posts, go back to Morphy's own. Your settings are kept, and when Premium starts again Morphy puts back the branding it saved. Discord doesn't let Morphy read an About Me, so Morphy only has one that was set with /branding after the Free plan was added.
  • Payments aren't refunded for partial periods, but if something went wrong, contact us and we'll make it right where we reasonably can.
  • We may change the price in future. If we do, we'll tell servers with Premium before the change applies to them.

4. The casino is make-believe

Casino money (for example "coins" or a server's own currency) is virtual and has no real-world value. It can't be bought with real money through Morphy, sold, traded for anything of value, or cashed out. Servers must not sell or reward casino money for real money.

  • break the law or Discord's rules;

  • collect personal information from people without their permission;
  • try to break, overload, reverse-engineer or get around limits in the bot.

We may stop providing Morphy to a server or user that does these things.

You also let us show your server's name, icon and member count, whether it has Premium or the Free plan, and a Join button if your staff add one, in the list on our website (section 7). You're responsible for what your server posts through Morphy.

7. The server list on our website

Morphy's website lists the servers that use Morphy. Every server is on it by default, on the Free plan or with Premium, except age-restricted servers. Servers on the Free plan show in a separate "Also using Morphy" group, below the servers with Premium. Each listing shows the server's name, icon and member count, plus a Join button if your staff add an invite with /showcase invite. Staff with the Manage Server permission can hide the server with /showcase off. Don't use your listing to mislead people, for example by copying another server's name or icon. We can take any server off the list. Section 4 of our Privacy Policy has the details.
