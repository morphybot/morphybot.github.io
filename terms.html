Morphy Terms of Service

Last updated: October 4, 2026

These terms cover your use of Morphy, a Discord bot. "We" and "us" mean the people who run Morphy. By adding Morphy to a Discord server or using its commands, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please remove Morphy from your server.

1. What Morphy is

Morphy is a Discord bot with a free plan for every server and a paid plan called Premium. It provides tools such as tickets, welcome messages, applications, invite tracking, giveaways, polls, info panels, points and community games with make-believe coins. Each server sets Morphy up for itself.

2. Who can use it

  • You must follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, including Discord's minimum age.
  • The person who adds Morphy to a server (or who manages it) is responsible for how it's set up and used in that server.

3. Free plan and Premium

  • Every server gets the Free plan: welcome messages and picture cards, goodbye messages, auto roles and role menus, plus the /setup, /help, /subscribe and /showcase commands. It has no time limit and needs no payment.
  • Premium adds the rest of Morphy, for example tickets, branding, community games with make-believe coins, applications, giveaways, invite tracking, anti-nuke and the do-not-post trap. It costs $9.99 USD per server for 30 days or $99.90 USD per server for 365 days (or the price shown when you pay).
  • Payments are made by hand for now (for example Cash App, PayPal or Venmo). Each one covers 30 days, or 365 days at the yearly price, and doesn't renew automatically. We check each payment by hand and switch Premium on within 24 hours. When paid time ends, Premium stays on for 3 more days. If you pay before your paid time ends, or during those 3 days, the new days are added to the old end date. We'll remind you before the time runs out.
  • If we offer card payments, they renew automatically until you cancel with /subscribe, and Premium stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • When Premium ends, the server goes back to the Free plan. Premium features pause, and the bot's name, picture, banner and About Me in that server, and the color and footer on its posts, go back to Morphy's own. Your settings are kept, and when Premium starts again Morphy puts back the branding it saved. Discord doesn't let Morphy read an About Me, so Morphy only has one that was set with /branding after the Free plan was added.
  • No refunds for time already paid, except for a billing mistake or where required by law. To report a billing mistake or ask about a refund, open a ticket in the Morphy support server or email us (section 12).
  • We may change the price in future. If we do, we'll tell servers with Premium before the change applies to them.

4. The games use make-believe coins

Morphy's community games, like blackjack, slots and roulette, use make-believe coins (or whatever a server calls its currency). Coins have no money value and can't be redeemed for anything. Members get them free inside the bot (a starting balance, earning them, or from staff and other members), never by buying them. A Premium payment pays for the bot's features. It doesn't buy coins, bets or prizes, and it doesn't change the odds of any game.

Coins must not be sold, bought with real money, cashed out, or traded for money, crypto, gift cards, goods, paid roles, paid services, game items or any other prize of value. Server staff must not hand out coins in return for a purchase, a donation or a paid membership. Giving coins to another member inside Morphy doesn't make them worth anything outside it.

Don't use Morphy to run paid-entry raffles, gambling for prizes, or any promotion that breaks the law. We may stop providing Morphy to a server that uses it this way.

5. Acceptable use

Don't use Morphy to:

  • break the law or Discord's rules;
  • harass, spam, scam or harm people;
  • collect personal information from people without their permission;
  • try to break, overload, reverse-engineer or get around limits in the bot.

We may stop providing Morphy to a server or user that does these things.

6. Your content

Messages, form answers, panels and other content you or your members put into Morphy stay yours. You give us permission to store and display that content only as needed to run Morphy for your server. You also let us show your server's name, icon and member count, whether it has Premium or the Free plan, and a Join button if your staff add one, in the list on our website (section 7). You're responsible for what your server posts through Morphy.

7. The server list on our website

Morphy's website lists the servers that use Morphy. Every server is on it by default, on the Free plan or with Premium, except age-restricted servers. Servers on the Free plan show in a separate "Also using Morphy" group, below the servers with Premium. Each listing shows the server's name, icon and member count, plus a Join button if your staff add an invite with /showcase invite. Staff with the Manage Server permission can hide the server with /showcase off. Don't use your listing to mislead people, for example by copying another server's name or icon. We can take any server off the list. Section 4 of our Privacy Policy has the details.

8. Availability

We work hard to keep Morphy online, but it's provided "as is". It may sometimes be unavailable, change, or have bugs. Discord itself can also have outages or change its rules, which can affect Morphy.

9. Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent the law allows, we aren't responsible for indirect or unexpected losses from using Morphy (for example lost data, lost members or lost income), and our total responsibility is limited to what you paid us for Morphy in the three months before the problem.

10. Ending

You can stop using Morphy at any time by removing it from your server. Payments made by hand don't renew, so there's nothing to cancel. We may end or suspend service if these terms are broken.

11. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. If the changes are important, we'll let server owners know (for example by DM or in the Morphy support server). Continuing to use Morphy after changes means you accept them.
