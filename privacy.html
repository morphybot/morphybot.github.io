Morphy Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what information Morphy, a Discord bot, collects, why, and what you can do about it. "We" and "us" mean the people who run Morphy.

1. What we collect

Morphy only keeps what it needs to work in your server.

About servers

  • Server ID and name, and the settings your staff choose (channels, roles, messages, templates, images you upload for welcome cards or branding).
  • Plan status (Free or Premium) and dates, and a log of each time we add, change or end Premium by hand.
  • Payment reports: when staff press I've paid in /subscribe, we keep who sent it (their Discord user ID and name), the plan, how they paid and the name or note on the payment, so we can match it to the payment. The report is also sent to us in a Discord message.
  • ARK game servers your staff add with /servers (Premium): the server's address and port, and what the game server shows to anyone who asks: whether it's online, its name, map, player count, player limit and version, checked every 2 minutes. We keep a history of whether it was online and how many players it had for 30 days. For ARK: Survival Evolved servers, /servers players asks the game server for the names of the players on it and shows them in Discord. We don't save player names.
  • Nitrado link, only if your staff link one with /nitrado link: the Nitrado token, saved encrypted and never shown again to anyone, your staff included (only Morphy's code opens it, to ask Nitrado); the game servers it can see (ID, name, game and slot count); the token's scopes and expiry date; and who linked it and when. Server status, player names and activity logs are read from Nitrado when they're shown, kept in memory for a few minutes at most, and not saved. With game logs on (/gamelogs), every 5 minutes Morphy also reads Nitrado's activity log and the newest part of each ARK server's ShooterGame.log file (joins, leaves, chat, kills and admin commands), and posts the new lines in the channels your staff picked, with player IDs (Steam, Epic, ARK IDs) cut out. Morphy saves only a fingerprint (a hash) of each line it handled, kept 30 days so a line is never posted twice, and how far into the file it has read. The lines themselves aren't saved, apart from the posts in your channels. Morphy reads no other file on your game server. If staff turn on review signals, Morphy also keeps a hash of a game account identifier (or a name-based key when the log has no identifier), the displayed player name, and observed join, leave, ban and session times. These observations expire 30 days after last observation and are removed when the Nitrado link is removed during cleanup. Signal deduplication records expire after 30 days. Forum thread IDs are saved to reuse the same threads. These flags do not establish account age or prove wrongdoing, and do not trigger punishment.
  • Daily server stats: how many members the server had, and how many joins, leaves, messages, commands, welcome messages and voice channel joins it had each day. These are counts only, with no message text and nothing about who did what. They're kept for 400 days, and staff can stop the counting with /stats tracking.
  • If we add card payments later, Stripe will handle them and we'll only receive a customer or subscription reference. We'd never see or store card numbers.

About members (stored by Discord user ID)

  • If staff enable Premium join-burst detection, we store server and member IDs and join times to count each join once across bot copies. Join receipts become eligible for removal after 24 hours. Alert records store the server, count, window, settings revision, time and delivery outcome, with no member list or message text. Finished alert records become eligible for removal after 30 days. Cleanup runs hourly in batches, so removal can take longer during a backlog. Unfinished alerts are checked before removal; uncertain sends are not repeated.
  • Moderation warnings, staff reasons, who recorded or cleared them, warning counts, and action records for timeouts, kicks, bans and link deletions. Action records keep their outcome and whether the mod-log was confirmed. They prevent duplicate events from repeating punishments. These records are kept until the server asks us to delete them. Deleted link text and URLs are not stored.
  • Who opened, claimed and closed each ticket, its type and the answers to its questions. Transcripts are posted to your server's transcript channel.
  • Answers to staff applications, and who accepted or denied each one.
  • Who invited whom, when people joined or left, and whether an account looked brand new (the invite tracker).
  • Casino coin balances, point totals, cooldown times and a history of changes.
  • XP totals, this week's XP, when each member last earned XP, and weekly XP rewards (if staff turn XP and levels on).
  • Poll votes, giveaway entries and suggestions.
  • Pets (/pet): each member's pet, with its name, kind, XP, food, how many times it was fed and played with, and when it was adopted, last fed and last played with.
  • Coin shop (/shop): the items and coupons staff make, and who made them. For each order: who bought it, the item, how many, the price in coins, any discount and coupon, the role it gave and whether Discord added it, and for a refund, when, who refunded it and the reason. We also keep which members used each coupon. Items and coupons staff remove are marked removed, not deleted, so old orders still show them.
  • Staff pay (/staffpay): Morphy works out each staff member's tickets and reviewed applications from the ticket and application records above, plus when each ticket was first claimed. For the monthly summary we keep the month, the channel and message ID of the post, and whether it went out. The summary and its spreadsheet file are posted in your server, not kept by us.
  • Hype rewards (/hype): for each member and reward type (name keyword, server tag, boost or invite), the season, when the last reward was given, how many times, and the coins given in total. For invite rewards, who invited whom and whether it paid out.
  • Guessing events (/event): each event's channel, type, answer, prize settings, who hosted it, who stopped it, who won, the message ID of the winning guess, the prize, the number of wrong guesses, and when it started and ended. For wrong guesses we keep only the message IDs, never the text.
  • Reminders (/remindme): the text you typed, when it's due and the channel it goes to (or that it's a DM), deleted once a send is confirmed or you cancel it. If a send cannot be confirmed, the reminder stays with an unknown outcome and is not sent again automatically.
  • Birthdays (/birthday set): the month and day only, never a year, until you run /birthday remove.
  • Sticky roles (if staff turn them on with /autorole sticky): the roles you had when you left the server, kept up to 90 days. Confirmed role restores are removed from the recovery record; refused restores stay for another attempt.
  • Embed builder (/embed): saved templates (the embed text, picture links and buttons staff wrote) and who made and last changed each one; one unfinished draft per staff member; and for each post, the channel, message ID, who posted it, the template used and when.

Messages

  • Custom-command settings contain staff-written replies. Delivery records contain the server, user and message IDs, time and whether the reply was sent or uncertain, so duplicate events cannot send twice. We do not store members' command text. Up to 100 delivery records older than 30 days are removed on each new reply; quiet servers can retain older records until the next reply or requested deletion.
  • Morphy reads messages in your server only to run features you turn on, for example Automod, the do-not-post trap and text commands like !bj or !links. Message text can be included in ticket transcripts and server-log posts in the channels your staff choose. Edited or deleted text can be logged only when it was available in Morphy's memory. Choose a private staff log channel, since its readers may not have access to the original channel. These logs are not a complete archive. Server-log delivery records contain hashes, event type, server ID, time and outcome, not message text. Records older than 30 days are removed in batches when new events arrive; quiet servers can retain them until another event or a deletion request.

Email

  • If you email Morphy's support address, we get your email address and what you wrote, and use them only to answer you.

Website visitors

  • Our website, morphybot.github.io, sets no cookies and has no trackers. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and its home page loads the server list from Morphy's server on Railway and the server icons from Discord, so GitHub, Railway and Discord see your IP address, like any website you visit. Discord's image server may also set a short-lived cookie of its own.

Dashboard

The dashboard is a separate site on Morphy's server on Railway, where staff sign in with Discord to change their server's settings. It isn't part of the website above. Like any website, Railway sees your IP address when you use it.

  • When you sign in, Discord tells us your Discord user ID, your name, your profile picture and the list of servers you're in, with your permissions in each. We save your user ID, name and picture, and the IDs of the servers on that list where Morphy is and you're the owner or have Manage Server. The rest of the list isn't saved. The access token Discord gives us is used once to read these and is never saved.
  • The dashboard sets three cookies it needs to work, and none for tracking or ads. __Host-morphy_session keeps you signed in. __Host-morphy_csrf proves that a change came from the dashboard's own page. __Host-morphy_oauth only exists while you sign in and lasts 10 minutes. We save only a scrambled copy (a hash) of each, never the cookies themselves.
  • You're signed out after 8 hours without using the dashboard, or 7 days after you signed in, whichever comes first. Sign out ends the session on that device, and Sign out on every device ends all of them.
  • For each sign-in, sign-out and change, the dashboard's change history keeps who did it (Discord user ID), the server, the page, what kind of change it was, when, whether it worked, the names of the settings changed, and the IDs of the roles, channels, members or items it touched. It never keeps what the settings were set to, any text you typed, pictures or tokens. Staff with Manage Server can see their server's history on the Activity page.
  • To limit how often someone can sign in or load pages, we count requests for a few minutes at a time. Sign-in counts use a scrambled copy (a hash) of your IP address made with a random key that changes every day, so the address itself isn't saved. Other counts go by session, Discord user ID and server.
  • Pictures you upload on the dashboard wait 10 minutes for you to save them, and are deleted if you don't.

2. How we use it

Only to run Morphy's features for your server, keep your Premium working, match payment reports to payments, list your server on our website (see section 4), prevent abuse, fix problems, and contact people about their plan (for example Premium renewal reminders to server owners by DM, or a note to whoever sent a payment report). We don't sell your information, and apart from the server list in section 4, we don't use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

  • Discord, because Morphy runs on Discord.
  • Railway (our hosting and database provider), which stores the data above for us.
  • Stripe, if we add card payments later, to process them.
  • Google (Gmail), which holds emails sent to our support address.
  • Nitrado, only if your staff link a Nitrado account: Morphy sends Nitrado your token to read your game servers' status, players and activity log, and, with game logs on, the end of ShooterGame.log. That file is downloaded from Nitrado's file server through a one-time link, and your token isn't sent with it. Morphy never changes anything on Nitrado.
  • Anyone who visits our website, which shows the server list in section 4 (name, icon, member count, whether it has Premium or the Free plan, and a Join link if your staff add one).
  • To check a tracked ARK server, Morphy asks the game server directly over the internet, and for ARK: Survival Ascended it downloads Studio Wildcard's public server list. We don't send either of them anything about your Discord server or its members.
  • Anyone the law requires us to share with.

4. The server list on our website

Morphy's website has a list of the servers using Morphy. Every server is on it by default, on the Free plan or with Premium, except age-restricted servers. Servers on the Free plan show in a separate "Also using Morphy" group, below the servers with Premium. The list shows each server's name, icon and member count, plus a Join button if your staff add an invite with /showcase invite. The icon's link has the server's Discord ID in it, because that's how Discord's image links work. Nothing else about the server or its members is shown.

Staff with the Manage Server permission can run /showcase off to hide the server. It drops off the list within about 5 minutes. /showcase on puts it back. We can also take a server off the list ourselves.

5. How long we keep it

  • Server settings and member data are kept until you ask us to delete them, including after Morphy leaves your server. Premium settings stay saved while the server is on the Free plan, so you don't lose your setup if Premium starts again.
  • Account-age gate records contain server and member IDs, join time, settings revision, action and delivery outcome. They contain no names or message text. Completed records are removed after 30 days by a periodic cleanup. Unfinished records stay until Morphy can resolve them or you ask for deletion.
  • Payment reports and the log of Premium time we add by hand are kept the same way, so we can answer billing questions.
  • A Nitrado link (the encrypted token and the saved server list) is deleted when staff run /nitrado unlink, which works on the Free plan too, or when Morphy is removed from the server.
  • If you'd like your server's data deleted, open a ticket in the Morphy support server or email us (section 10). We delete it within 30 days of the request.
  • A tracked ARK server's history is kept for 30 days. Removing the server with /servers remove deletes it and its history right away.
  • Daily server stats are kept for 400 days, then deleted.
  • A pet is deleted when its owner runs /pet release or staff remove it.
  • A reminder is deleted when its send is confirmed or the member cancels it. An unknown outcome stays until cancelled and is not retried automatically. A birthday stays until the member removes it. Sticky roles are deleted after 90 days, after confirmed recovery, or when staff turn sticky roles off.
  • An unfinished /embed draft expires after 30 days without a change, and is deleted the next time anyone saves a draft. Saved templates stay until staff delete them.
  • Coin shop orders, staff pay post records, hype rewards and guessing events are kept like other member data, until the server asks us to delete them.
  • Dashboard records are deleted on their own: the change history after 90 days, a session a day after it ends, request counts once their few minutes are up, and each day's IP key the day after. Morphy clears these out when it starts and then once an hour, so a record can stay up to an hour past its time.
  • Casino money, points and similar data can also be reset by your server's staff at any time.

6. Your choices

  • Server owners can remove Morphy at any time and ask us to delete the server's data.
  • Staff can hide your server from the list on our website with /showcase off.
  • Members can ask us what we hold about them, or ask for it to be deleted, by opening a ticket in the Morphy support server (some records, like a server's own ticket transcripts, are controlled by that server).

7. Children

Morphy is for people 13 or older, or older if your country sets a higher minimum age for Discord. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you think we hold a younger person's information, tell us (section 10) and we'll delete it.

8. Security

Data is stored with our hosting provider and access is limited to running Morphy. Nitrado tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, using a key kept only in our hosting settings, apart from the database. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are important, we'll let server owners know.

10. Who is responsible

Morphy is run under the name Morphy from Missouri, United States. That's who holds the data described here.
