Questions and answers

What is Morphy?

A Discord bot with Free and Premium tools, set up separately in each server.

How much is it?

The Free plan costs nothing and doesn't run out. Premium is $9.99 USD for 30 days or $99.90 USD for 365 days per server. Run /subscribe in your server to see how to pay, then press I've paid. We check each payment by hand and switch Premium on within 24 hours.

What's free, and what needs Premium?

Every server gets custom commands, moderation and Automod, welcome messages and picture cards, goodbye messages, auto roles, role menus, chat and voice XP levels, a server log, reminders, birthdays, sticky roles and sticky messages for free, plus /setup, /help, /subscribe, /showcase and /stats. No card needed. Premium adds tickets with close requests and saved replies, branding, daily rewards and games with make-believe coins, XP boosts and multipliers, applications, giveaways, invite tracking, boosts, points, panels, ARK server tracking, ARK game-log threads and review flags, anti-nuke, the do-not-post trap and more.

What can Automod check on the Free plan?

Words, spam, mentions, caps, duplicates, invites and zalgo: Free, up to 10 rules. Premium adds regex, files, per-rule exemptions and 25 rules. Use /automod or the safety page.

Can Morphy keep a server log?

Yes, Free. Use /logs setup. Keep logs private: cached message text can appear. They are not a complete archive.

Can members earn XP in voice?

Yes, Free: /levels-setup voice checks once a minute, not exact talking time. Boosts, multipliers and coin rewards need Premium.

Are reminders and birthdays free?

Yes: /remindme, /reminders and /birthday set. Birthdays save month and day only. Sticky roles and messages are also Free.

Does each server pay on its own?

Yes. Premium covers one server, even if you own more than one.

How do I set it up?

Invite Morphy with the Add Morphy free button, then run /setup and follow the setup guide.

What happens when Premium ends?

The server goes back to the Free plan. Custom commands, moderation and Automod, welcome messages and picture cards, goodbye messages, auto roles, role menus, chat and voice XP levels, a server log, reminders, birthdays, sticky roles and sticky messages keep working, and so do /setup, /help, /subscribe, /showcase and /stats. Tickets, the games, invite tracking, anti-nuke, the do-not-post trap and the other Premium features pause, and the bot's name, picture, banner and About Me in your server go back to Morphy's own look. Your settings are kept. When you renew, the Premium features come back and Morphy puts back the branding it saved. Paid time doesn't renew by itself, and the server owner gets reminders by DM before it ends.

Can I get a refund?

No refunds for time already paid, except for a billing mistake or where required by law. If something went wrong with a payment, open a ticket in the Morphy support server. If you can't use Discord, email morphybot.support@gmail.com.

Are the games real-money gambling?

No. Blackjack, slots, roulette and the other games use free, make-believe coins. Nobody can buy coins through Morphy or cash them out, and the Terms don't allow trading coins or winnings for anything of value. The games come with Premium, which pays for the bot. It doesn't buy coins or prizes, and it doesn't change the odds.

Does Morphy touch my game server?

It only reads what staff set up. Premium /servers add tracks public ARK status. /nitrado link adds game logs; its token is encrypted and /nitrado unlink deletes it. Morphy can't restart, stop or change a server.

Does Morphy need Administrator?

No. Morphy asks for 16 permissions, and Administrator isn't one of them. They're listed below, each with what it's for.

Can I hide my server from the list on Morphy's website?

Yes. Staff with Manage Server use /showcase off; it takes about 5 minutes. /showcase on restores it. Free servers have their own group. Age-restricted servers are never listed.

Where do I get help?

Open a ticket in the Morphy support server. If you can't use Discord, email morphybot.support@gmail.com.
